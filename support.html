Quick answers.

Does this form send the message automatically?

No. JS Spec is currently hosted as a static website. Pressing the button opens an email draft in your configured email app. You need to press Send there.

Can I view the source code?

Yes. Public project repositories are linked above and from the portfolio. Some projects are still under development.

How can I report a problem with OneNudge?

Choose “OneNudge question” in the form, describe the steps you took and what happened, and include any error message. Please don't send passwords or other sensitive information.
